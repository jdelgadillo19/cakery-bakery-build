# CAKERY BAKERY VISUAL STYLE GUIDE v1

## GAME IDENTITY

Cakery Bakery is the cozy dessert district of the Gojito universe.

The game should feel:
- warm
- comforting
- inviting
- flavorful
- handcrafted
- whimsical

The bakery aesthetic is intentionally softer and cuter than other Gojito games.

It must follow the Gojito Executive Style Guide. (~/Projects/gojito-platform/docs/styleguide/styleguide.md)

---

# THEMATIC VISUAL LANGUAGE

Primary themes:
- fresh baked goods
- cozy kitchens
- pastry displays
- warm lighting
- small-town bakery charm
- playful food stylization

Secondary themes:
- recipe discovery
- crafting mastery
- customer rush energy
- cheerful workplace atmosphere

---

# COLOR PALETTE

Primary tones:
- cream
- cinnamon brown
- warm tan
- butter yellow
- strawberry red
- mint green

Accent tones:
- blueberry
- chocolate brown
- soft peach

Avoid:
- neon saturation
- cold industrial tones
- excessive pink dominance

---

# CHARACTER DESIGN

Characters should feel:
- expressive
- approachable
- hardworking
- energetic

Preferred traits:
- apron silhouettes
- rounded but readable shapes
- practical bakery attire
- active gameplay poses

Avoid:
- overly frilly costumes
- doll-like posing
- hyper-feminized aesthetics

---

# FOOD DESIGN

Food should appear:
- delicious
- slightly exaggerated
- highly readable
- stylized rather than realistic

Preferred traits:
- soft highlights
- simplified texture detail
- iconic silhouettes
- warm appetizing colors

---

# ENVIRONMENT DESIGN

Bakery environments should feel:
- cozy
- slightly magical
- tidy but lived-in
- warm and inviting

Preferred materials:
- wood
- tile
- soft metal
- paper packaging
- warm lighting

Avoid:
- excessive clutter
- hyper-real kitchen realism
- sterile commercial appearance

---

# UI STYLE

UI should resemble:
- recipe cards
- bakery signage
- pastry packaging
- café chalkboard elements

However:
all UI must remain:
- bold
- readable
- game-first
- interaction-focused

Gameplay readability overrides decoration.
